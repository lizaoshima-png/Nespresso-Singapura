Nespresso Brazil runs its full customer journey on Blip — Commerce · Care · Campaigns, operated end to end with a dedicated Professional Services squad (developers and conversational designers) and weekly governance with the CRC. Singapore asked to reuse the same ecosystem.

The moment of Nespresso Singapore

A channel ready to do much more

  • WhatsApp is not yet an official Nespresso channel in Singapore — the team is working with Meta to implement it and wants to reuse the Brazil ecosystem.
  • About half of sales happen on the website; email marketing, SMS, media and boutiques are the other official touchpoints.
  • The ambition is broader: promotions, cart recovery, engagement and steering behaviour — not only selling through WhatsApp.
  • Singapore first, with Malaysia and Thailand under evaluation for the same regional team.
  • The open question is not the front end — it is how the back end connects: ERP, Nessoft, carriers and the agent desk.

Solution · use cases

The Brazil menu, phased for Singapore.

Two ways to get there — the choice mostly depends on how much of the Nespresso Brazil journey can be reused as it is.

Path A · Replicate Brazil

Copy the Brazil flows, localise and go live

If Singapore can reuse the Nespresso Brazil flows as they are — adapting language, catalog and local integrations — implementation effort and time to go-live are significantly lower, since the journeys, design and logic are already proven in production.

Path B · Phased build

If a direct copy is not possible

When local systems, processes or priorities require a different design, we suggest the phased approach below: start where the value is clearest and the integrations are lightest, then expand as systems connect.

Why Blip as the AI orchestration layer

Orchestrate what Nespresso already has — and what it will add.

01

Middleware for the Intelligent Contact

Blip sits between the consumer's messaging app and Nespresso's agents and systems — the brand's single conversational front door.

02

Open protocols

A2A and MCP connect agents and tools on both sides, so systems like Genesys, ERP or Braze plug in instead of being rebuilt.

03

No model lock-in

LLM-agnostic by design: each agent uses the model that fits the task, including Nespresso's own when relevant.

04
